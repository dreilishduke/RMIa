The explorer uses 60 per group and one shared SD, so it starts at t = 2.41 with df = 118. The paper had 58 and 61 people (df = 117) and reports t = 2.38. The small difference is rounding.

Challenges

  1. Keep the real gap and SD. What is the smallest group size that still gives p < .05?
  2. Set 300 per group. How small can the gap get and still be “significant”? Is that gap worth caring about? Check d.
  3. Double the SD. What happens to t? Why would a less reliable memory test do this?
